## How to turn on GitHub Pages (super easy)
1. Go to your repo: https://github.com/M1KUPRO67/My-website
2. Click **Settings** (top menu)
3. On the left, click **Pages**
4. Under "Source", choose **Deploy from a branch**
5. Branch: **main**  +  Folder: **/ (root)**
6. Click **Save**

Wait 1–2 minutes, then open the link above. Done!
